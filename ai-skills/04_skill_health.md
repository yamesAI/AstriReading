## SECTION A: CONSTITUTIONAL STRENGTH

**Purpose:** Assess how robust the native's physical constitution is — their baseline health and recovery capacity.

### Three Pillars of Physical Constitution

**Pillar 1 — 1st House Ruler (Physical Body)**
The planet ruling the Ascendant sign governs the native's physical body and vitality.
- Dignified (domicile/exaltation): Strong physical constitution, good resistance
- Peregrine: Average constitution, moderate resilience
- Afflicted (detriment/fall): Weaker constitution, slower recovery, more susceptible

**Pillar 2 — Sun (Vital Spirits)**
The Sun governs the vital force — the life energy animating the body.
- Dignified Sun: Strong vital energy, good recuperative power
- Afflicted Sun: Depleted vital energy, fatigue-prone, reduced life force
- Combust planets near Sun: Those life areas may also be depleted

**Pillar 3 — Moon (Humors and Fluids)**
The Moon governs the body's humors — blood, lymph, moisture, digestion.
- Dignified Moon: Healthy fluid systems, good emotional-physical integration
- Afflicted Moon: Hormonal imbalances, digestive issues, emotional sensitivity affecting health

### Constitutional Assessment Scale

| Pillars Strong | Constitutional Level |
|---------------|---------------------|
| All 3 dignified | **Robust** — recovers quickly, strong immune response |
| 2 of 3 dignified | **Good** — generally healthy with one area of vulnerability |
| 1 of 3 dignified | **Moderate** — requires more care, slower recovery |
| None dignified | **Delicate** — chronic vulnerability, must tend health carefully |

## SECTION B: ILLNESS SIGNIFICATORS

**Purpose:** Identify which planets indicate health challenges for this specific chart.

### The 6th House Ruler (Always Significant)
The 6th house naturally governs illness, so its ruler ALWAYS has a role in health assessment.

**Judge 6th ruler by essential dignity:**
- Strongly dignified (domicile/exaltation): The native may experience significant illness — the "illness factor" is powerful
- Peregrine (no dignity, no affliction): **Best for health** — illness factor is weak, fewer and milder ailments
- Strongly afflicted (detriment/fall): The illness factor is also strong — serious debility creates health issues

**NOTE:** Both a very strong AND a very weak 6th ruler indicate illness. Peregrine = healthiest 6th house position.

### Mars as Illness Significator
Mars becomes a significator of illness IF any of these apply:
- Mars is in the 1st house
- Mars conjuncts the 1st house ruler
- Mars squares or opposes the Sun
- Mars squares or opposes the Moon
- Mars squares or opposes the 1st house ruler

**When Mars is an illness significator:** Look for acute conditions, accidents, fevers, inflammation, stress-related illness, migraines, surgical issues.

### Saturn as Illness Significator
Saturn becomes a significator of illness IF any of these apply:
- Saturn is in the 1st house
- Saturn conjuncts the 1st house ruler
- Saturn squares or opposes the Sun
- Saturn squares or opposes the Moon
- Saturn squares or opposes the 1st house ruler

**When Saturn is an illness significator:** Look for chronic conditions, bone/joint problems, depression, fatigue, slow-developing illness, digestive blockages, skin conditions.

## SECTION C: ILLNESS TYPE IDENTIFICATION

**Purpose:** Identify the most likely areas of health vulnerability.

### Step-by-Step Illness Type Formula

1. Identify which planets are illness significators (6th ruler + Mars if applicable + Saturn if applicable)
2. For each significator, note:
   a. The planet's natural body/illness rulership (see table below)
   b. The SIGN the planet occupies (governs body region)
3. Note which of the three constitutional pillars is afflicted (1st ruler / Sun / Moon) and its sign
4. Cross-reference: illness significator + its sign + afflicted health pillar + health pillar's sign = likely body system

### Planetary Health Rulerships
| Planet | Natural Health Domain |
|--------|----------------------|
| Saturn | Joints, bones, teeth, depression, chronic/fatigue conditions, blockages, skin |
| Jupiter | Liver, excess conditions, weight, blood sugar, over-expansion |
| Mars | Head, face, accidents, burns, inflammation, fever, acute illness, stress |
| Sun | Heart, vital spirits, stamina, spine |
| Venus | Kidneys, adrenal glands, hormones, reproductive system, pleasure-excess related |
| Mercury | Nervous system, lungs, arms, hands, respiratory system |
| Moon | Stomach, digestion, breasts, fluids, hormones, immune response |

### Sign Body Regions (see Foundation document for full table)
Key assignments: Aries=head, Taurus=throat/neck, Gemini=lungs/arms, Cancer=stomach/chest,
Leo=heart/spine, Virgo=intestines, Libra=kidneys/lower back, Scorpio=genitals/urinary,
Sagittarius=hips/thighs/liver, Capricorn=knees/bones, Aquarius=calves/circulation,
Pisces=feet/lymphatic/immune

### Illness Pattern (6th House Sign Quality)
**Sign quality on 6th house cusp indicates illness pattern:**
- **Cardinal/Moveable sign:** Illnesses are acute but don't last long; native recovers relatively quickly
- **Fixed sign:** Illnesses are prolonged, stubborn; conditions become chronic if not addressed
- **Mutable/Common sign:** Illnesses come and go; condition appears, improves, then returns

**6th Ruler Retrograde:**
- Retrograde 6th ruler = illnesses are recurrent; chronic or returning conditions

## SECTION D: SPECIAL HEALTH PATTERNS

### The Self-Caused Illness Pattern
IF the planet ruling the Ascendant ALSO rules the 6th house → The native is often the cause of their own health problems (lifestyle choices, stress, self-neglect, pushing too hard).

### Water Sign Accumulation
IF multiple planets (3+) occupy water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) → Susceptibility to fluid-related conditions: lymphatic congestion, hormonal imbalances, immune dysfunction, emotional-physical sensitivity.

### Malefic in 1st House
- Saturn in 1st: Chronic health challenges from youth; cold/dry constitution; bone, joint, or depressive conditions
- Mars in 1st: Accident-prone, prone to fevers and inflammation; excess heat conditions

### Dignified vs. Afflicted 6th Ruler in Illness
- **Well-dignified 6th ruler** with malefic aspects to health pillars = notable, sometimes unusual illness
- **Peregrine 6th ruler** with no major aspects to health pillars = few and mild illnesses

## INTERPRETIVE NOTES

1. **Never diagnose.** This is a constitutional tendency reading, not medical advice. Always note: "This reading identifies tendencies from a traditional astrological perspective. Please consult a qualified healthcare provider for any health concerns."

2. **Frame health reading positively.** The goal is awareness and prevention, not fear. A "delicate constitution" doesn't mean constant illness — it means being more intentional about health.

3. **Peregrine 6th ruler = good news.** Make sure to explicitly tell the client when their 6th ruler is peregrine — this is a genuinely positive health indicator worth highlighting.

4. **Dignity of malefics matters.** A dignified Mars in 1st house is far less problematic than an afflicted Mars. A dignified Mars = warrior energy that protects; afflicted Mars = accident-prone, inflammatory.

5. **The 8th house in health** relates to surgery, chronic/terminal illness, and death — a topic of great sensitivity. For standard health readings, focus on the 6th house. The 8th is included in inputs but should only be mentioned with great care, typically only to note if the native is constitutionally long-lived.
