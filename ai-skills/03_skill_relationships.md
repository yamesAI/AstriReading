# SKILL 03: Relationships, Marriage & Children
## Source: Lesson 8 — Lilly's *Christian Astrology*, Gadbury's *Doctrine of Nativities*

---

## INPUTS REQUIRED FROM USER

```
- Native's gender (male or female)
- Full chart: all planetary positions (sign, house, degree)
- Essential dignity of each planet
- 7th house cusp sign and ruler
- 8th house cusp sign and ruler (spouse's finances)
- 5th house cusp sign and ruler (children)
- 11th house cusp sign (secondary children house)

- Planets IN the 5th house
```

## SECTION A: MARRIAGE & COMMITTED RELATIONSHIPS

### A1 — Number of Marriages/Significant Relationships

**For Female Charts — use the SUN:**
Count the number of aspects the Sun makes to OTHER planets.
- Each aspect (conjunction, sextile, square, trine, opposition) = 1 significant relationship
- 1 Sun aspect = 1 primary marriage
- 2 Sun aspects = 2 significant committed relationships
- 3+ aspects = multiple relationships over the lifetime

**For Male Charts — use the MOON:**
Count the number of aspects the Moon makes to OTHER planets.
Apply same formula as above.

**Stability Indicator (both genders):**
Check the Sun-Moon aspect:
- Sextile, trine, or conjunction: Marriage(s) stable, unlikely to end in divorce
- Square or opposition: Significant tension in marriage; separation or divorce likely at some point
- No Sun-Moon aspect: Instability in committed relationships; marriage less central to life

**Additional Indicator:**
- Multiple planets in 7th house = additional relationships or marriage experiences
- North Node conjunct 7th cusp = karmic positive partnerships, beneficial marriage
- South Node conjunct 7th cusp = karmic challenges in partnerships, repeated patterns

### A2 — Spouse/Partner Character

**Step 1 — Identify the Primary Relationship Significators:**

*For female chart:*
- Primary natural significator of husband = **Sun** and **Mars**
- Secondary = 7th house ruler and planets in 7th

*For male chart:*
- Primary natural significator of wife/partner = **Moon** and **Venus**
- Secondary = 7th house ruler and planets in 7th

**Step 2 — 7th House Ruler Analysis:**
1. Identify the ruler of the 7th house cusp sign
2. Note: sign, house placement, essential dignity, all aspects
3. Planets IN the 7th house: note same for each

**Step 3 — Character Matrix (7th Ruler and Planets in 7th)**

**By Planet:**
| Planet | Character Traits |
|--------|----------------|
| Saturn | Serious, reserved, older, hardworking, traditional, may be depressive or restrictive |
| Jupiter | Generous, philosophical, enthusiastic, big-hearted, possibly excessive |
| Mars | Energetic, aggressive, passionate, direct, can be angry or controlling |
| Sun | Self-focused, generous, likes recognition, natural authority figure |
| Venus | Artistic, attractive, pleasure-loving, social, affectionate |
| Mercury | Intelligent, communicative, versatile, possibly deceptive or overly clever |
| Moon | Emotional, changeable, nurturing, sensitive, moody |

**By Sign Quality:**
| Quality | Partner Trait |
|---------|--------------|
| Cardinal (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn) | Initiates, assertive, starts things, can be impulsive |
| Fixed (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) | Stubborn, reliable, doesn't change easily, enduring |
| Mutable (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces) | Flexible, changeable, may be inconsistent |

**By Sign Element:**
| Element | Partner Character |
|---------|-----------------|
| Fire | Enthusiastic, emotional, warm, impulsive, dramatic |
| Air | Intellectual, cool, detached, social, ideas-focused |
| Water | Sensitive, emotional, intuitive, nurturing, changeable |
| Earth | Practical, grounded, steady, methodical, material-focused |

**Step 4 — Relationship Quality Assessment:**

Check the 7th ruler's dignity and its aspect to the 1st ruler (native):
- 7th ruler dignified + trine/sextile to 1st ruler → Harmonious partnership, good qualities in partner
- 7th ruler afflicted + square/opposition to 1st ruler → Tension, arguments, difficult dynamic
- 7th ruler dignified but NO aspect to 1st ruler → Good partner but lives independently; parallel lives
- 7th ruler afflicted + no aspect → Partner is difficult but doesn't directly engage with native much

**Problem Indicators in 7th House:**
| Placement | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|
| Saturn in 7th | Loneliness within marriage, depression, isolation, or significant age difference |
| Mars in 7th (afflicted) | Arguments, quarrels, physical aggression, passionate conflicts |
| South Node in 7th | Karmic relationship patterns that repeat; partner brings karmic lessons |
| Venus retrograde as 7th ruler | Partners are unreliable, avoid commitment, or love runs hot-cold |
| Mercury badly afflicted in 7th | Communication breakdowns, arguments, deception |
| Neptune conjunct 7th ruler | Native idealizes or is deceived by partners; projection of fantasy |

**Step 5 — Spouse's Financial Status (8th House = Partner's Resources):**
1. Identify 8th house ruler
2. If dignified: partner is financially stable or provides financial benefit
3. If afflicted: partner has financial difficulties or causes financial stress

## SECTION B: CHILDREN & FERTILITY

### B1 — Fertility Assessment

**Fertile vs. Barren Classifications:**

**Planets:**
| Planet | Fertility Rating |
|--------|----------------|
| Jupiter | Fertile — prolific |
| Venus | Fertile — prolific |
| Moon | Fertile — moisture |
| North Node | Fertile (Jupiter/Venus nature) |
| Mercury | Neutral — takes on nature of planets it aspects |
| Sun | More barren than fertile (excess heat) |
| Mars | Barren |
| Saturn | Barren |
| South Node | Barren (Saturn/Mars nature) |

**Signs:**
| Fertility | Signs |
|-----------|-------|
| Fertile | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces |
| More Fertile than Barren | Taurus, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius |
| More Barren than Fertile | Aries, Capricorn |
| Barren | Gemini, Leo, Virgo |

**Step 1:** Identify 5th house ruler. Cross-reference planet fertility + sign fertility.

**Combined Fertility Assessment:**
- Fertile planet in fertile sign, dignified = **Strong fertility**
- Fertile planet in barren sign = **Mixed — fertility possible but with effort or delay**
- Barren planet in fertile sign = **Mixed — fertility possible but not natural inclination**
- Barren planet in barren sign, afflicted = **Weak fertility or few children indicated**

**Step 2:** Check planets IN the 5th house. Each fertile planet in 5th supports; each barren planet inhibits.

**Step 3:** Check 11th house cusp sign (secondary fertility house) — fertile or barren classification.

**Step 4:** Check 1st house and its ruler (parent's constitution) — fertile or barren planet/sign ruling 1st.

**Step 5:** Count fertile vs. barren indicators across all factors — majority determines overall fertility.

---

### B2 — Number of Children (Tendency)

- Count planets in or conjunct the 5th house: more planets = tendency toward more children
- Check 5th house cusp sign quality:
  - Fixed sign: fewer children (fixed = resistant to multiple)
  - Mutable/Common sign: variable, children interspersed
  - Cardinal/Moveable sign: supports multiple conceptions
- Moon aspects count (secondary indicator of children number)

**NOTE:** These are tendencies, not certainties. Modern circumstances (birth control, life choices) always modify.

---

### B3 — Children's Gender (Tendencies)

**Masculine indicators:**
- Masculine planets (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Sun) ruling or in 5th
- Masculine signs (Fire & Air) on 5th cusp

**Feminine indicators:**
- Feminine planets (Venus, Moon) ruling or in 5th
- Feminine signs (Earth & Water) on 5th cusp

Count masculine vs. feminine indicators — majority suggests tendency toward more boys or more girls.

---

### B4 — Parent-Child Dynamic

Check Moon and 1st ruler (parent significators) for aspects to 5th ruler and planets in 5th (children):
- Sextile/trine → harmonious parent-child relationship
- Square/opposition → friction, conflict, different natures
- No aspect → neutral, parallel lives

Check for malefics in key parenting houses:
- Saturn/Mars/South Node in 1st: Parent may be difficult or creating challenges
- Saturn/Mars/South Node in 5th: Children may be challenging or have difficulties

## INTERPRETIVE NOTES

1. **Gender-neutral reading:** If the client hasn't specified their gender or orientation, use gender-neutral language. The significator rules (Sun for one partner's significance, Moon for another's) still apply based on chart dynamics even in same-sex partnerships.

2. **Never predict divorce definitively.** A square between Sun and Moon shows *tendency toward tension* — not guaranteed divorce. Say "there may be significant challenges in sustained commitment" rather than "you will divorce."

3. **Fertility is a tendency, not a verdict.** Never tell someone they cannot have children based on the chart. Say "the chart shows less natural inclination toward" or "fertility may require more intentional effort."

4. **The 7th house describes the PARTNER, not the relationship quality.** A well-dignified 7th ruler shows the partner is a good person even if the aspect to the 1st ruler is tense. Conversely, an afflicted 7th ruler in good aspect to the 1st ruler may mean the native accepts a difficult partner.
