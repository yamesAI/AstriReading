## QUALITY STANDARDS FOR THE READING

### Tone
- Warm, intelligent, and direct — not vague, hedged, or overly mystical
- Professional but human — this is a personal reading, not an academic paper
- Specific — every statement should reference a chart factor (planet, sign, house, aspect, dignity)
- Constructive — challenges are opportunities for awareness, not verdicts

### Format
- Use headers and subheaders for navigation
- Use **bold** for key terms and planet names
- Keep each section to a readable length: 3-6 paragraphs per major section
- Total reading length: approximately 1,200–2,000 words

### What to Avoid
- Generic statements that could apply to anyone ("You are sensitive and complex")
- Modern psychological jargon mixed with traditional terminology
- Over-hedging ("This MIGHT suggest...") — be clear and precise
- Overwhelming the client with every planet and aspect — synthesize, don't catalog
- Predicting death, divorce, or catastrophe directly (frame as tendencies, not certainties)

### What to Prioritize
- The most essentially dignified planet in the chart — this is the chart's greatest strength
- The most afflicted planet — this is the chart's key learning area
- The rising sign and Ascendant ruler — these define the person's fundamental nature
- The most occupied house — this life area receives the most cosmic attention
- The tightest aspects (partile or near-partile) — these are the strongest chart factors
