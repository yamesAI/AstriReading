## ASPECTS (Traditional Ptolemaic Only)

| Aspect | Degrees | Nature |
|--------|---------|--------|
| Conjunction | 0° | Positive or negative depending on planets involved |
| Sextile | 60° | Benefic (imperfect love) |
| Square | 90° | Malefic (imperfect hatred) |
| Trine | 120° | Strongly benefic (perfect love) |
| Opposition | 180° | Strongly malefic (perfect hatred) |

**Moiety Rule (orbs):** Each planet has an orb. Add half the orb of each planet to determine if they are in aspect.
- Saturn: 9° | Jupiter: 9° | Mars: 7° | Sun: 15° | Venus: 7° | Mercury: 7° | Moon: 12°

**Applying vs. Separating:** Applying aspects are stronger in natal (planets moving toward perfection).
**Partile:** Same degree = very strong. **Platic:** Different degrees but within orb = moderate.

## HOUSE SYSTEM (Regiomontanus — Required for Traditional Work)

### House Meanings
| House | Primary Significations |
|-------|----------------------|
| 1st | Native's person, appearance, health, vitality, self |
| 2nd | Money, movable possessions, income, financial resources |
| 3rd | Siblings, neighbors, short journeys, communication, local environment |
| 4th | Father, real estate, home, inheritance, end of life |
| 5th | Pleasures, children, creativity, games, romance, gambling |
| 6th | Illness, servants, employees, small animals, daily work |
| 7th | Spouse/partner, open enemies, business partnerships, the public |
| 8th | Death, other people's money, dowry, legacies, transformation |
| 9th | Long journeys, philosophy, religion, higher education, foreign lands, dreams |
| 10th | Career, honor, reputation, public standing, mother, magistrates |
| 11th | Friends, hopes, wishes, benefactors, favor, social networks |
| 12th | Secret enemies, imprisonment, affliction, hidden matters, occult, large animals |

### House Strength (Lilly's Scale)
- Angular (1st, 10th): +5 | Angular (4th, 7th): +4 | Succedent (11th): +4
- Succedent (2nd, 5th): +3 | Succedent (8th): -2 (malefic despite succedent)
- Cadent (9th): +2 | Cadent (3rd): +1 | Cadent (6th): -2 | Cadent (12th): -5

**Five Degree Rule:** A planet within 5° of the NEXT house cusp (by sign order, counterclockwise) partakes in the strength and affairs of that next house. It does NOT change house placement, only adds house association.

**Almuten of a House:** The planet with the most essential dignity points at the exact degree of the house cusp.

**Turned Houses:** To examine affairs of a house relative to another person, count the relevant house from their significator house (e.g., spouse's money = 2nd from 7th = 8th house).

## ACCIDENTAL DIGNITIES & DEBILITIES

### Benefic Accidental Factors
- In angular house (especially 1st, 10th)
- Direct motion
- Fast in motion (especially Moon)
- Free from Sun's beams (more than 17° from Sun)
- Cazimi (within 17' of Sun center) — major fortification
- Conjunct North Node
- Conjunct benefic fixed star

### Debilitating Accidental Factors
- Combust (within 8°30' of Sun) — serious affliction
- Under the Sun's Beams (within 17° of Sun) — weakening
- Retrograde — unreliability, things going backward
- In cadent house (especially 6th, 12th)
- Conjunct South Node
- Conjunct malefic fixed star (especially Algol at ~26° Taurus)

### Key Fixed Stars (Modern Positions — ~2026)
- Algol (Caput Medusae) ~26° Taurus — most malefic star, violence, beheading
- Pleiades ~29° Taurus — weeping, grief
- Aldebaran ~9° Gemini — honor if dignified, violence if afflicted
- Regulus ~29° Leo — honor, success, royal favor
- Spica ~23° Libra — wealth, success, benefic
- Antares ~9° Sagittarius — obstinacy, violence, similar to Mars/Jupiter

### Planetary Relationships to Sun
- **Cazimi:** Within 17' — planet fortified, in heart of Sun
- **Combust:** Within 8°30' — planet seriously weakened
- **Under Beams:** Within 17° — planet weakened but functional
- **Oriental:** Planet rises before Sun (in earlier degree/house)
- **Occidental:** Planet rises after Sun (in later degree/house)

## DAY vs. NIGHT CHART DETERMINATION
- **Day Chart:** Sun in houses 7 through 12 (above horizon at birth)
- **Night Chart:** Sun in houses 1 through 6 (below horizon at birth)

This affects triplicity rulership counting (though in natal work, all three triplicity lords receive +3).

## SIGN CLASSIFICATIONS

### Sign Quality (Modality)
- **Cardinal/Moveable:** Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn — initiating, quick to start/finish
- **Fixed:** Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius — stubborn, slow to change, enduring
- **Mutable/Common:** Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces — changeable, indecisive, wavering

### Commanding vs. Obeying Signs
- **Commanding:** Aries through Virgo — more assertive, rational
- **Obeying:** Libra through Pisces — more compliant, emotional

### Masculine vs. Feminine Signs
- **Masculine (Fire & Air):** Aries, Gemini, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius
- **Feminine (Earth & Water):** Taurus, Cancer, Virgo, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces

### Fertility Classification
- **Fertile:** Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces
- **Barren:** Gemini, Leo, Virgo
- **More Fertile than Barren:** Taurus, Libra, Sagittarius, Aquarius
- **More Barren than Fertile:** Aries, Capricorn
