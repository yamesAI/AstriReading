## SECTION A: TEMPERAMENT (Gadbury's 6-Factor Method)

**Purpose:** Determine the native's dominant psychological character — their innate way of being.

### Step 1 — Rising Sign Element
Note the element of the Ascendant sign. Record its hot/cold/wet/dry quality.

### Step 2 — Ascendant Ruler Element
Find the planet ruling the Ascendant sign. Record its hot/cold/wet/dry quality.

### Step 3 — Planets Aspecting the Ascendant
For each planet conjuncting, squaring, trining, sextiling, or opposing the Ascendant:
- Record the elemental quality of each such planet

**Oriental/Occidental Elemental Adjustments (Gadbury):**
| Planet | Oriental | Occidental |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Saturn | Cold & Moist | Cold & Dry |
| Jupiter | Hot & Wet | Hot & Wet (no change) |
| Mars | Hot & Dry | Hot & Dry (no change) |
| Venus | Hot & Wet | Cold & Wet |
| Mercury | Hot & Dry | Hot & Wet |

### Step 4 — Moon and Planets Aspecting the Moon
Determine Moon's elemental quality by phase:
- New Moon → First Quarter (waxing crescent/square): Hot & Wet
- First Quarter → Full Moon (waxing gibbous): Hot & Dry
- Full Moon → Third Quarter (waning gibbous/square): Cold & Dry
- Third Quarter → New Moon (waning crescent): Cold & Wet

For each planet aspecting the Moon, also record its elemental quality (apply Oriental/Occidental adjustment).

### Step 5 — Sun's Season
- Spring (Aries, Taurus, Gemini): Hot & Wet
- Summer (Cancer, Leo, Virgo): Hot & Dry
- Autumn (Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius): Cold & Dry
- Winter (Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces): Cold & Wet

### Step 6 — Almuten Figuris
The most dignified planet overall (highest total essential dignity points). Record its elemental quality.

### Tally & Determine Dominant Temperament
Count all "Hot" votes, all "Cold" votes, all "Wet" votes, all "Dry" votes.
The dominant pairing (most Hot/Cold AND most Wet/Dry) determines temperament:

| Result | Temperament | Character Profile |
|--------|-------------|------------------|
| Hot & Dry | **Choleric** | Energetic, willful, natural leader, aggressive, impatient, likes change, results-oriented |
| Hot & Wet | **Sanguine** | Friendly, happy, social, extroverted, charming, easily distracted, needs novelty |
| Cold & Wet | **Phlegmatic** | Calm, contemplative, shy, introverted, thoughtful, stable, avoids conflict, comfort-seeking |
| Cold & Dry | **Melancholic** | Serious, hardworking, pessimistic, isolated, stubborn, analytical, detail-oriented, introverted |

**Synthesis Rule:** A mixed temperament (e.g., Hot&Dry + Hot&Wet almost equal) = describe both as "primarily X with Y qualities." A temperament is a tendency, not an absolute.

## SECTION B: MANNERS (Behavioral Expression)

**Purpose:** Identify how the native expresses themselves in social interactions.

### Significator of Manners — Hierarchical Selection (use first that qualifies)

1. **Planets conjunct the Ascendant** (within 5°) OR planets in the rising sign OR intercepted in 1st house → USE as significator
2. **Planets conjunct the Moon** (within 3°) OR conjunct Mercury (within 3°) → USE as significator
3. **Ascendant ruler** IF it aspects its own dispositer → USE Ascendant ruler
4. **Almuten of Mercury and the Moon** (planet with most dignity points at the degrees of Mercury and Moon combined)
5. **Default:** Use Ascendant ruler

**CRITICAL:** Only use Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, or Mercury as significator. Never use Sun or Moon.

### Interpret the Significator

**If dignified (domicile or exaltation):**
> The native presents as: calm, controlled, thoughtful, fair in judgment, intelligent, can be elegant, poetic, or diplomatic. Maintains composure.

**If afflicted (detriment or fall):**
> The native can be: aggressive, domineering, quarrelsome, revengeful, rash, willful, or has temper/anger management issues.

**Modify by Planet:**
- **Saturn dignified:** Serious, reserved, solitary, disciplined, keeps promises
- **Saturn afflicted:** Depressed, isolated, bitter, harsh, restrictive
- **Jupiter dignified:** Generous, wise, fair, enthusiastic, ethically motivated
- **Jupiter afflicted:** Hypocritical, overpromises, exaggerates, grandiose
- **Mars dignified:** Courageous, decisive, direct, energetic, protective
- **Mars afflicted:** Aggressive, angry, violent temper, provokes conflict
- **Venus dignified:** Charming, artistic, gracious, social, attractive energy
- **Venus afflicted:** Vain, indulgent, manipulative through beauty/flattery
- **Mercury dignified:** Clever, articulate, witty, good communicator
- **Mercury afflicted:** Deceptive, overly clever, sarcastic, tricky

**Fixed Star Conjunctions (within 1° of significator):**
- Algol ~26° Taurus: Prone to losing control, association with violence or extremity
- Regulus ~29° Leo: High ambition, desire for honor, susceptible to hubris
- Spica ~23° Libra: Grace, gifts, favorable reception by others
- Antares ~9° Sagittarius: Obstinate, rash, tendency to excess

## SECTION C: WIT & INTELLIGENCE

**Purpose:** Describe the native's type and quality of mind.

### Step 1 — Compare Mercury and Moon Strength
Assess essential dignity + accidental strength of each.

**Mercury stronger than Moon:**
> Reason typically governs emotion. Native makes decisions with head more than heart.

**Moon stronger than Mercury:**
> Emotion typically overrides reason. Native feels deeply and acts from feeling.

**Equal strength:**
> Variable — sometimes rational, sometimes emotional depending on the situation.

### Step 2 — Check Commanding vs. Obeying Signs
- Mercury in Commanding sign (Aries–Virgo): Adds rationality
- Moon in Obeying sign (Libra–Pisces): Softens emotional control

### Step 3 — Mercury Speed and Condition
- **Fast Mercury (>1°/day):** Quick learner, picks up ideas rapidly even if otherwise afflicted
- **Slow Mercury (<1°/day):** Deep thinker, needs time to process — not unintelligent, just thorough
- **Combust Mercury:** Occasional failures of judgment, blind spots, poor decisions at times
- **Retrograde Mercury:** Lapses in reasoning, revisits decisions, self-doubt about thinking

### Step 4 — Mercury-Moon Aspect
- Trine or sextile: Harmonious integration of mind and emotion; pleasant to converse with
- Square or opposition: "Rugged fancy" — more imaginative than logical; creative but argumentative
- Conjunction: Merged — more emotional if Moon strong, more rational if Mercury strong
- No aspect: Moderate, assess by individual strength

### Output Template
```
TYPE OF MIND: [rational/emotional/variable]
LEARNING STYLE: [quick/deep/methodical]
STRENGTHS: [e.g., "fast learner, adaptable thinker"]
WATCH FOR: [e.g., "occasional judgment lapses when Mercury is combust"]
```

## IMPORTANT INTERPRETIVE NOTES

1. **Never pathologize.** Melancholic is not "depressed" — it is serious, disciplined, and deep. Choleric is not "angry" — it is energetic and driven. Frame everything as strength first.

2. **Contradictions are normal.** A Scorpio rising (Mars-flavored = assertive) with Venus as manners significator (dignified = gracious) = someone who appears calm and charming but has strong internal will. Say this explicitly.

3. **Context matters.** Describe the tendency, not the certainty. Use language like "tends toward," "inclines to," "may express as."

4. **Personalize.** Don't just recite the temperament label — connect it to specific planets and positions so the reading feels individually crafted.
