## SECTION A: INCOME (2nd House Analysis)

**Purpose:** Describe the native's financial life — sources of income, financial strength, and timing of prosperity.

### Factor 1 — Ruler of the 2nd House
1. Identify the planet ruling the 2nd house cusp sign.
2. Assess its **essential dignity** (domicile +5 / exaltation +4 / triplicity +3 / term +2 / face +1 / peregrine 0 / fall -4 / detriment -5)
3. Note which **other houses** this planet rules (e.g., if it also rules the 6th, income may come through work/service/health)
4. Note its **house placement** (angular = active and obvious / succedent = moderate / cadent = background/hidden)
5. Assess all **aspects** to this planet (benefic aspects add, malefic aspects harm income)

**Income Interpretation by Dignity:**
- Dignified (domicile/exaltation): Good income, earns well, financial strength
- Peregrine: Income is moderate, irregular, or inconsistent
- Afflicted (detriment/fall): Financial challenges, losses, inconsistent income

### Factor 2 — Planets in the 2nd House
For each planet IN the 2nd house:
1. Note its natural rulership (what does this planet govern by nature?)
2. Note all other houses it rules — these show additional income sources
3. Assess its essential dignity within the 2nd house
4. Note aspects to planets outside the 2nd

**Interpretation:** Planets in the 2nd house add their nature to income. Saturn in 2nd = income from hard work or scarcity. Jupiter in 2nd = financial expansion, generosity. Venus in 2nd = income from arts, beauty, relationships.

### Factor 3 — Jupiter (Natural Ruler of Wealth)
1. Note Jupiter's sign and essential dignity
2. Note its house placement
3. Note its aspects
- Jupiter dignified and in positive house: Financial opportunities, generosity, growth
- Jupiter afflicted: Financial overexpansion, poor judgment about money, losses through bad deals

### Factor 4 — Part of Fortune
1. Note the Part of Fortune's sign and house placement
2. Note if any planets conjunct the Part of Fortune
3. Assess the dignity of the PoF's dispositor (the ruler of the sign PoF occupies)
- Dignified dispositor: Part of Fortune actively brings good fortune
- Afflicted dispositor: Fortune is present but blocked or inconsistent

### Income Source Identification
Use planetary natural rulerships to identify the MOST LIKELY sources of income:

| 2nd Ruler or Planet in 2nd | Income Source |
|---------------------------|--------------|
| Saturn | Hard work, agriculture, mining, construction, serious/difficult work, time |
| Jupiter | Finance, law, education, religion, large institutions, research |
| Mars | Military, security, engineering, manufacturing, fire-related, competitive fields |
| Sun | Government, authority, management, public roles |
| Venus | Arts, beauty, fashion, entertainment, food, relationship-based work |
| Mercury | Communications, technology, writing, sales, teaching, accounting |
| Moon | Travel, public-facing work, hospitality, women's industries, food service |

### INCOME TIMING — Bonatti's Life Thirds Method

**Purpose:** Identify which life stage is most financially prosperous.

**Step 1:** Determine day or night chart.
**Step 2:** Find the 2nd house cusp sign's element (Fire/Earth/Air/Water).
**Step 3:** Look up the triplicity rulers for that element and chart type:

| Element | Day Chart (Young→Middle→Old) | Night Chart (Young→Middle→Old) |
|---------|------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| Fire | Sun → Jupiter → Saturn | Jupiter → Sun → Saturn |
| Earth | Venus → Moon → Mars | Moon → Venus → Mars |
| Air | Saturn → Mercury → Jupiter | Mercury → Saturn → Jupiter |
| Water | Venus → Mars → Moon | Mars → Venus → Moon |

**Life Stages:**
- 1st ruler governs ages 0–25 (youth/early career)
- 2nd ruler governs ages 25–50 (prime working years)
- 3rd ruler governs ages 50+ (later life)

**Judge each period by the essential dignity of that triplicity ruler in the natal chart:**
- Dignified ruler = prosperous period
- Peregrine ruler = moderate period
- Afflicted ruler = difficult period financially

## SECTION B: CAREER (10th House Analysis)

**Purpose:** Describe the native's likely vocation, career success, and professional path.

### Primary Method — 10th House Ruler Analysis

1. Identify the planet ruling the 10th house cusp sign.
2. Assess its **essential dignity**
3. Note its **house placement**
4. Note all **other houses** it rules
5. Assess all **aspects** to the 10th ruler

**Career Interpretation by Dignity:**
- Dignified 10th ruler: Career success, honor, professional recognition
- Peregrine 10th ruler: Career is functional but lacks distinction
- Afflicted 10th ruler: Career struggles, changes, lack of recognition, reversals

**Planets IN the 10th house:** Each adds its nature to the career. Multiple planets in 10th = career has many dimensions. A benefic (Jupiter/Venus) in 10th = fortunate career. A malefic (Saturn/Mars) in 10th = hard-won success through difficulty.

---

### Medieval Triple Significator Method (Secondary — Career Type)

**Purpose:** Find the ONE planet that most specifically identifies the career field.

**Only examine: Mercury, Venus, Mars** (not Sun, Moon, Saturn, Jupiter)

Apply these criteria in order; use the FIRST that matches:

**Priority 1:** Is Mercury, Venus, or Mars:
- In an angular house (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th)?
- AND essentially dignified (domicile or exaltation)?
- AND free from serious affliction (not combust, not heavily squared by malefic)?
→ IF YES: This planet is the career significator. **Highest career success indicated.**

**Priority 2:** Does Mercury, Venus, or Mars:
- Rule the 10th house?
- AND is essentially dignified?
→ IF YES: This planet is the career significator. **Good career success indicated.**

**Priority 3:** Does Mercury, Venus, or Mars:
- Apply to an aspect with the Moon?
- AND is free from malefic affliction?
→ IF YES: This planet is the career significator. **Moderate career success indicated.**

**Priority 4:** Does Mercury, Venus, or Mars:
- Rise before the Sun (oriental)?
→ IF YES: This planet is the career significator. **Variable career success indicated.**

**If none qualify:** Use 10th house ruler as sole indicator.

**Career Field by Significator:**

| Significator Planet | Career Field |
|--------------------|-------------|
| Mercury | Technology, communications, writing, information, teaching, business, analysis |
| Venus | Arts, entertainment, beauty, design, hospitality, fashion, relationships |
| Mars | Engineering, military, athletics, medicine/surgery, law enforcement, manufacturing |

## SECTION C: SYNTHESIS — Income & Career Integration

**Synthesis Rules:**
1. The 2nd ruler and 10th ruler often share a planet (one planet rules both). This shows income and career are tightly linked.
2. Check if the 2nd ruler and 10th ruler **aspect each other:**
   - Trine/sextile: Income and career reinforce each other positively
   - Square/opposition: Income and career work against each other (good career but poor pay, or good pay but unfulfilling career)
   - No aspect: They operate independently

3. **The aspect's planets' dignities determine outcome:**
   - 2nd ruler dignified aspecting afflicted Saturn (6th ruler): Income suffers from illness/service issues
   - 2nd ruler dignified aspecting dignified Saturn (10th ruler): Hard work pays off financially

## INTERPRETIVE NOTES

1. **Context modifies everything.** A person born into wealth with the same chart as someone born into poverty will experience income differently. Acknowledge that the chart shows potential and tendency, not guaranteed outcome.

2. **Same aspect, different dignity = different meaning.** The 2nd ruler squaring Saturn in the 6th could mean: (a) income suffers from illness if Saturn is afflicted, or (b) income comes FROM health work if Saturn is dignified. Always check dignity before concluding.

3. **The 10th house in traditional astrology is actually the mother** (not the father as in modern). For career, the 10th house is correct but understand that the parent who was the authority figure may also show here.

4. **Don't over-specify.** The chart shows general career fields, not "you should be a data analyst at a fintech company." Give the field (Mercury careers) and characteristics, not the exact job title.
